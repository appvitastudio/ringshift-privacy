English

Ring Shift works offline and has no account. Game progress, settings and statistics are stored only on your device; we do not collect, receive or sell them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland a consent form (Google UMP) lets you accept or refuse personalised ads; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The game is not directed at children under 13. Data deletion: data deletion page.
